## The four slides that carry the talk

- **4 — A hazard is never an icon.** The whole design philosophy in one
  comparison. Give it the full two minutes and let the room search the
  screenshot themselves.
- **6 — Decoys.** What separates this from a click-the-hotspot trainer.
- **14 — 9,587 → 994.** A real measured problem, solved with a named technique.
- **16 — What went wrong.** Finding your own bugs reads as competence, not
  weakness. Do not apologise through it.

## Running behind?

Cut in this order — each is self-contained:

1. Slide 13 (stack) — credibility, not story
2. Slide 9 (scoring detail) — the numbers are on screen anyway
3. Slide 10 (locations) — a nice-to-have finding

Never cut 4, 6, 16 or the demo.

## Demo — 2 minutes, rehearsed

Have the game **already loaded** in another window. Do not load it in front of
them.

1. Walk the Main Storage Hall
2. Find the falling boxes
3. **Flag a decoy on purpose** — the coned-and-signed spill — and let the game
   explain why that is wrong. This is the moment that sells it.
4. Show the results screen

Resist exploring. Finish on results and take questions.
