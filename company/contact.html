While you are here

What this website does with your data

Nothing is collected

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising tags and no third party scripts of any kind. The pages load from this site and nowhere else.

No cookies, so no banner

We set none, which is why you were not asked to agree to any. A consent banner is only needed when there is something to consent to.

The form sends nothing

It opens your own email program with the text filled in. Until you press send in that program, what you typed has not left your computer.

When you do write to us we keep your email only for as long as the conversation needs, and we do not add anyone to a mailing list. The product itself is separate and has its own account and data handling, which is described inside it.
