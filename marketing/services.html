Services

Five things we do. Every one of them is work we have shipped on a real brief and can show you running, which is also why the list is short. If you need something that is not here we will say so rather than learn it on your budget.

01 · Training

Interactive 3D training simulations

Training that puts the learner in the situation and scores the decision, running in an ordinary browser tab. We use this where recall in a room is a poor proxy for judgement on a floor: safety induction, equipment handling, site familiarisation.

The learner walks the space, acts, and is told why they were right or wrong at the moment it still means something. The output is a score somebody can act on, not a certificate of attendance.

What is included

  • A walkable 3D environment built to your site, not a stock template
  • Hazards or scenarios staged as real objects, never floating icons
  • A guided mode and a scored mode, with difficulty that changes the task
  • A debrief naming everything they missed, with the correct control
  • Understanding checked as well as spotting, with an end of round quiz

02 · Product

Web applications that run anywhere

Software people can open without asking anyone's permission first. No install, no app store, no plugin and no headset, because the thing that kills a rollout is rarely the software and usually the thirty minutes of setup in front of it.

We build offline first by default. The product is cached on first visit, works with the network unplugged, and also ships as one self contained file you can put on a USB stick and carry into a building with no Wi-Fi at all.

What is included

  • One codebase for desktop, tablet and phone
  • Installable to the home screen or the desktop
  • A single file offline build, around 1 MB
  • Work queued while offline and sent when the connection returns
  • Automatic deployment, so what you review is what is live

03 · Security

Accounts and records you can trust

If a result is going into somebody's training file it has to be worth something. We build the account layer and the record store together so that a finished record cannot be quietly improved afterwards, by the person who sat the test, by their supervisor, or by us.

We are careful about what that claim means. Storage can be made unchangeable; a copy held on a device can only be checked, so we tag those and show when one has been edited rather than pretending it cannot happen.

What is included

  • Email and password sign in, with passkeys and authenticator app two factor
  • Row level security, so a person can only ever read their own records
  • A results table with no update route at all, for anybody
  • A supervisor view: who has trained, how they scored, what is missed most
  • Delete my account and my data, because people are entitled to ask
  • Content Security Policy, HTTPS, a frame guard and an idle lock for shared machines

04 · Assurance

Testing, documentation and handover

The part most small teams skip, and the reason their work stops being used six months later. Every behaviour that matters has an automated test behind it, and the whole suite runs on every change before anything can be published.

You get the defect log as well as the passes. We would rather hand you a written list of what is still wrong than let you find it yourself in front of your own staff.

What is included

  • An automated test suite that blocks a broken build from going live
  • A test plan with the manual checks, results and the defect log
  • A user guide written for the person on the floor
  • A change log tying every change back to who asked for it
  • A plain statement of what has not been tested yet

05 · Design

Interface design and accessibility

A training tool is used once by each person, under mild stress, often on somebody else's laptop. It has to be obvious the first time or it is not used at all, so we design for the first ninety seconds rather than for the expert user who does not exist yet.

Accessibility is part of that and not a later pass: a workforce is mixed, and an induction nobody can get through is an induction that did not happen.

What is included

  • Screen designs reviewed against recognised usability heuristics
  • Keyboard, mouse and full touch control, with adjustable sensitivity
  • Reduced motion, invert look, and colour that never carries meaning alone
  • Readable at phone width without a separate mobile version
  • Plain English throughout, including the error messages

Working with us

How an engagement runs

Four stages. You can stop after any of them and keep everything produced up to that point, including the source.

StageWhat happensWhat you get
Understand We sit with the people who will use it and watch the job being done. A written brief in your words, agreed before anything is built.
Show A rough but running version, early, with the hard part built first. Something you can click, and a cheap moment to change direction.
Build Short cycles with a demonstration at the end of each one. Working software at every step, tested, with a change log.
Hand over Documentation, the test record, and the honest list of limitations. A product that does not depend on us being available.

We are a student team, so we scope by project rather than by day rate, and we are straight about capacity: three people, and a university timetable around it. If a deadline is not realistic we will tell you before you commit to it, not afterwards.
