About the team

Three people, one product, and a client who can open it in his own browser. This page says who did what, so you know who you would be working with rather than which stock photograph we chose.

Why we exist

We build training that people actually finish, and remember on the floor a week later.

Almost every organisation has training nobody wanted and nobody recalls. The failure is rarely the content. It is that the training happens in a room and the decision happens somewhere else, under time pressure, with a forklift coming. We move the training to where the decision is.

How we work

Three things we will not trade away

Honest about limits

We document what we have tested and what we have not. Screen readers, Firefox, Safari and phone hardware are on our own untested list right now, and they are on it in writing. A training product that overstates itself is teaching the wrong lesson before it starts.

Runs on what people have

No headsets, no installs, no procurement cycle. If it does not open on the laptop already in the supervisor's office, it will not get used, and a product that is not used has no value however good it is.

Built, not licensed

Geometry, textures, audio, the QR encoder, the one time password code: written from the specification. Nothing we ship depends on a vendor we cannot see inside, and nothing we ship phones home.

The context, stated plainly

We are a student team

The Codecrafters was formed for CET257 Enterprise Project at the University of Sunderland. Beat The Hazard was built against a live brief from a logistics operator, and the demonstration linked from this site is the working product, not a mock up.

We say this on the front of the conversation rather than at the end of it. It sets what you can expect from us: three people, a university timetable alongside the work, and a scope we will agree honestly instead of promising a deadline we cannot keep.

What it does not change is how the work is done. The test suite, the documentation, the security review and the handover are the same ones we would want if we were paying for this.
